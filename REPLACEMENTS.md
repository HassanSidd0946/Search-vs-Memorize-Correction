**Tolerance notation**: draft.md uses plain Unicode symbols that `build_latex.py`'s `escape_special()` already converts for some characters (`−`→`$-$`, `×`→`$\times$`). It has **no rule for `±`** yet — GENERATOR_FIXES.md's G4 proposes adding one. Every `±` below assumes G4 lands first; until then, writing `±` into draft.md will pass through as a raw Unicode character of unverified rendering behavior. **Sequencing dependency, not yet resolved**: this map should not be applied until G1 and G4 (GENERATOR_FIXES.md) are fixed, or every occurrence here will either be silently reverted (G1) or render as an unconverted symbol (G4).
