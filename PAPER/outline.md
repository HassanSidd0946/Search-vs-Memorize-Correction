## Abstract sketch (three-part structure, per explicit instruction)

**(1) How a mislabeled dataset produced an uninterpretable 70.78% headline.** `EVENT_ID` (`run_data_engine_on_modal.py`) mapped four structurally distinct marker types — Encode-phase trial onset, Test-phase Target/Distractor/New-Lure recognition onset — into one binary class label per task. 75% of each class's epochs were recognition-test responses, 37.5 percentage points of which were responses to items never studied under either condition. The original 70.78% figure is arithmetically correct and scientifically meaningless as a claim about Search-vs-Memorize decoding. (L037.)

**(2) The diagnostic protocol that found and confirmed the mislabeling.** Composition arithmetic against real per-subject marker indices (not an assumed flat split) showed every prior "temporal drift"/"rest-break" pseudo-effect in this audit's history was actually an encode-vs-test-phase composition effect — 13/13 checked mappings fit (L038). A direct encode-vs-test decode (R1(b)) then cleared its own pre-registered joint-confirmation threshold (`post_cal_balanced`=0.8668 > 0.85, L049), **formally withdrawing** the entire drift/rest-break-discontinuity mechanistic account (F-DRIFT through F-DRIFT-G) that the withdrawn prior draft was built around.

**(3) The corrected contrast's own genuine, validated, but modest, pre-calibration finding.** Re-epoching on encode-phase trials only (the one epoch class with an unambiguous, uncontaminated, one-per-behavioral-trial label; 50/task/subject, 2,900 total, R2) yields `pre_cal_balanced`=0.5737 (`post_cal_balanced`=0.7296, not itself validated by the checks below — see Limitations, L052) — clearing a shuffled-label null computed on this exact dataset at both 30-shuffle (L042: null mean=0.5010, SD=0.0137) and 500-shuffle resolution (L054: null mean=0.4994, SD=0.0108, empirical p=0.0000 raw / 0.0020 add-one-corrected), not attributable to any single subject (L053: leave-one-out jackknife over the full 29-row table, LOO-mean range [0.5674, 0.5815], no exclusion drops the mean below the null's 0.5223 CI boundary).

**Effect size, stated plainly, not rounded up (per explicit instruction, H2/L047):** the raw elevation is **~7.3–7.4 percentage points above a ~50% chance floor** — real and subject-generalizable, but modest in absolute terms, smaller than the withdrawn contrast's own ~19–21pp calibration-driven lift (L009) and far smaller than any of the drift family's onset-driven step-changes (L025). Cohen's d against the (very tight, 29-fold-averaged) null distribution is large (5.3–6.9) — this reflects how tight a null built from averaging over 29 LOSO folds naturally is, **not** a dramatic raw effect, and the abstract must state both numbers side by side rather than lead with d alone.

**Conclusion sketch:** a label-dictionary bug produced an uninterpretable headline number; correcting it recovers a real but modest cross-subject pre-calibration signal, independently validated four ways. Contribution: the composition-arithmetic diagnostic protocol generalizes to any blocked multi-phase paradigm with an under-examined marker/event dictionary — independent of whether it uncovers a null (as the withdrawn framing initially found) or, as here, recovers a real signal that a labeling bug had hidden.

### 1. Introduction
- Motivate the general problem: EEG/BCI paradigms with multi-phase blocks (encode + recognition-test, here) and hand-written marker-code dictionaries are common; a dictionary error that silently merges phases is easy to make and easy to miss, because the resulting classifier still trains, still reports plausible-looking numbers, and still passes ordinary sanity checks (L037's `EVENT_ID` bug passed every check in this project's own pipeline until the marker structure was inspected directly).

### 4. The diagnostic protocol as a reusable recipe — updated for the corrected-contrast investigation

- Extract as a standalone, generalizable checklist, independent of this dataset, **updated from the withdrawn draft's version** to foreground what actually caught and fixed this project's own bug rather than the withdrawn drift-detection recipe:
  1. When a blocked/multi-phase paradigm's marker dictionary is hand-written, verify every code's real-world meaning against the raw behavioral records directly (exact-count cross-referencing) — do not assume a marker-code comment is accurate.
  2. Before trusting any pseudo- or real-contrast classifier result in a multi-phase design, compute the real per-subject COMPOSITION of each class directly from marker indices — this diagnostic requires no classifier training and can rule in or out a labeling confound before spending any compute on a model.
  3. When a composition-based account is proposed, require a joint criterion (composition arithmetic AND a direct decode of the suspected confound) before declaring it confirmed — either alone is suggestive, not decisive (R1(a) alone would not have been sufficient; R1(b)'s 0.8668 is what closed it).
  4. Once a corrected contrast is defined, validate any resulting signal against a null computed on the EXACT corrected dataset (not a differently-composed prior null) — C1/C4's central discipline point.
  5. Check robustness to single-subject influence (jackknife, C3) and to a structural confound the corrected design still permits (here: block-order/parity, D2) before treating a validated signal as fully characterized.
  6. Carry forward the withdrawn draft's items unchanged where still applicable: report pre-calibration accuracy alongside any calibrated number; harden every classification report with class balance/majority-rate/balanced accuracy.

### 5. Limitations

- **Post-calibration validity is untested by C1/C3/C4/D2.** All four robustness checks (including the now-landed D2) tested only the pre-calibration axis. R2's own `post_cal_balanced`=0.7296 (L052) inherits none of that validation, and this project's own standing Tier-1 finding (post-calibration numbers are fundamentally few-shot subject-adaptive, not zero-shot) applies to it exactly as it did to every withdrawn number. (L048/H3, item 1; L052.)
- **Task-instruction vs. other encoding-phase difference (block order, per-subject stimulus set) is substantially, though not completely, addressed.** F-STIM and F-PARITY-WITHIN's existing findings do not transfer to R2 (computed on the old contaminated dataset, under the withdrawn drift account) — D2 is the direct test computed on R2 itself. **D2 result: both parity groups (odd/Search-first n=14, even/Memorize-first n=15) individually clear their own within-group shuffled-label null** (odd: 0.6205 vs. null CI upper bound 0.5276; even: 0.5582 vs. null CI upper bound 0.5223) — since position and label are inversely mapped between the two groups, a pure position confound cannot explain same-direction elevation in both. **This narrows, but does not close, the limitation**: D2 rules out a simple position confound, not every possible non-task-instruction explanation (see the effect-size-asymmetry bullet immediately below, which D2 itself raises as a new, unresolved sub-question). (L048/H3, item 2; L051.)
- **D2's parity-group effect-size asymmetry is unresolved.** Odd's gap above its own null (0.1162) is roughly 2x even's (0.0597) — both real, both individually significant, but at n=14/15 this asymmetry could plausibly be small-sample variation rather than something with a real cause (e.g., a genuine difference in decodability between Search-first and Memorize-first subjects). **Stated here as its own limitation, not folded into the task-instruction-vs-position question above** — explicitly not pursued with a further dedicated control run, per instruction, given the group sizes involved. (L051.)
- **Effect size is modest in absolute terms.** ~7.3–7.4pp above chance — carried forward from the Abstract/H2 so it is never understated in this section relative to how it's stated up front. (L047.)
- **Single-seed basis (seed=42) throughout the R1–D2 investigation** — a deliberate, disclosed cost tradeoff, not a 5-seed protocol; carried over from the withdrawn draft's own limitation.
- **Pre-F3 alignment dependency** — carried over unchanged from the withdrawn draft; still applies, since R1–D2 all reuse the same pooled-only EA family for internal consistency with the original investigation.
